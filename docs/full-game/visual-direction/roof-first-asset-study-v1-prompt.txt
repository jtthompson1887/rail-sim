Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original visual direction asset study for a 2D railway management game. A single spacious sheet of separate overhead illustrated objects on a genuinely transparent background, not a production-ready sprite atlas.
Primary request: Rich illustrated railway assets with an absolutely straight-down orthographic camera. Camera is 90 degrees directly above every object. This is a 2D plan view, not an isometric view. Show horizontal top surfaces and roofs only. No vertical building facades, no broad train side walls, no tree trunks.
Subjects on this one sheet, each isolated with generous transparent space and never overlapping:
1. One recognizable modern fictional British/European diesel freight locomotive, long rectangular roof seen strictly from above, two cab roof ends, dark cab window-edge hints, cooling fan grilles, vents, engine access panels and modest original muted maroon/yellow livery. Believable 20-metre body proportions, buffers and couplings as small end details. No real brand or lettering.
2. One overhead flatbed freight wagon loaded with stacked timber beams secured with neat straps. The visible timber tops and wagon deck must clearly convey its load.
3. One overhead flatbed freight wagon loaded with steel sections secured to the deck. The metal top surfaces and discrete supports must clearly convey its load. Both wagon bodies about 14 metres long and the same railway width as the locomotive; tiny couplings align along the long axis. Do not draw rails beneath the vehicles.
4. One compact brick flour-mill building group viewed in plan: dominant slate and weathered metal roof shapes with ridge lines, subtle roof vents/skylights, two small round grain-silo tops, and a restrained paved loading court at ground level. Around 35 metres by 25 metres overall, correctly larger than the locomotive. No visible brick walls or facades: the identity comes from the industrial footprint, roofs and loading apron.
5. One small passenger station asset viewed strictly from overhead: a fitted slender platform, a modest canopy roof, a small brick station building roof, clear platform edging and a few tiny bench tops. Platform roughly 45 metres long, station footprint larger than a train wagon. No vertical canopy columns shown, no elevation perspective, no train and no tracks.
6. One compact group of three broadleaf tree crowns seen directly from above, irregular lobed overlapping foliage tops in natural meadow/olive/forest greens. Each crown about 5 to 7 metres wide, smaller than a wagon. No trunks and no triangular side-view trees.
Style: Mature original hand-painted 2D illustration for a premium overhead railway game. Crisp outer silhouettes and clear grouped details at small scale. Gentle brush texture within roofs and foliage, restrained edge wear. Paint the objects as flat overhead sprites, not 3D renders. Cohesive limited natural colours: slate grey, muted maroon, small warm yellow accents, brick-inspired roof context, timber brown, cool steel grey, earthy paving and layered greens.
Composition: One wide study sheet; separated subjects arranged neatly in open transparent space. All are from the same exact vertical camera and share coherent scale. The long vehicle axes are vertical on the sheet so their roof shapes read immediately. Building roofs and platform can be aligned horizontally. No labels, no captions, no UI, no border, no diagrams.
Lighting and transparency: Soft painted tonal variation, no cast shadows at all; detached shadows will be handled by the game. Background must have actual alpha transparency. Do not paint checkerboard squares, white backing or a ground rectangle. Loading court belongs only to the mill asset; all other surrounding space is transparent.
Constraints: The output is a style study to assess camera, silhouettes and material treatment, not an implemented game or finished atlas. Original fictional assets only. Strict plan view is more important than cinematic beauty.
Avoid: Isometric perspective, oblique camera, visible wall fronts, train side elevations, vertical trunks, long shadows, photographed textures, 3D plastic, dense noise, toy cartoon exaggeration, pixel art, real logos, letters, numbers, watermarks, background scenery.
