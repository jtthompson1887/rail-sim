Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original art-direction concept for a railway management game, a single 16:9 whole-game scene. This is a visual development illustration, not an implemented screenshot.
Primary request: Rich illustrated overhead world, with detailed modern trains and landscapes. A polished and welcoming fictional British/European region where the player designs a railway, rehearses its services, and transforms the region.
Scene/backdrop: A coherent riverside market town amid gently rolling farmland and woodland. A winding river with natural irregular banks, one railway bridge, hedged grain fields, an established town of brick and stone houses, and a small newly built neighbourhood. A working brick flour mill with roof vents and grain silos, a believable freight yard, and a passenger station integrate into the same landscape.
Subject: Railway is the visual anchor: one continuous curving double rail line across the world, a properly connected passing loop, realistic smooth turnouts, a freight siding reaching the mill, and a station with two fitted platforms and a modest canopy. A recognisably modern diesel freight locomotive pulls visible covered grain hoppers. A distinct two-car modern regional passenger train stands alongside a station platform. Roof detail, windows, couplings, rails, sleepers, ballast, platform edges, loading apron and a few tiny passengers should read clearly at this scale. Every train sits on track, track gauge and wheel proportions remain consistent, and track does not run through buildings.
Style/medium: Original premium hand-painted game illustration, production-minded modular asset look, coherent fine brush texture and crisp silhouettes. Illustrated overhead terrain and detailed roof-oriented train assets, not a photorealistic rendering. Enough natural variation to feel alive without visual clutter.
Composition/framing: Wide 16:9 orthographic overhead map, camera nearly straight down with only slight vertical elevation reveal; roofs dominate buildings, tree crowns are seen from above. World occupies at least 88 percent of the image. A medium zoom that lets the viewer read trains, platforms, town blocks, mill operation and countryside together. Landscape extends naturally beyond all four edges, no diorama pedestal. Keep railway infrastructure unobscured.
Lighting/mood: Warm clear late-morning light with soft short shadows consistently cast in one direction; inviting but grounded. Gentle river highlights, believable earthy building materials, quieter background terrain than infrastructure.
Color palette and materials: Restrained natural meadow and olive greens, warm dry field ochres, slate roofs, weathered red brick, cool river blue-grey, pale stone, muted steel. Hand-painted grass variation, layered shrubs, patchy woodland crowns, gravel and ballast. Train liveries use modest original accents, no real brands.
Interface: A compact thin warm off-white top status strip with only 'Rail Sim', '£284,600', and '1×'; a small tidy floating bottom toolbar with simple legible line icons and labels 'Build', 'Services', 'Projects', 'Plans'. One modest cream contextual project card, titled 'New neighbourhood', with two concise progress rows 'Materials 12 / 16' and 'Passengers 44 / 60'. Calm typography, balanced spacing, understated shadows, no ornamental frames. UI remains peripheral and never hides the principal trains or station.
Constraints: Modern diesel/electric-era fictional region, original architecture and fictional unbranded trains. Railway engineering should be plausible, smooth connected geometry with continuous rails and clear platform fit. This scene communicates an art direction, not detailed simulation accuracy. No reference copying.
Avoid: Strong isometric angle, toy city, exaggerated 3D bevels, cartoon tree symbols viewed from the side, circular facility markers, huge floating labels, photo textures, pixel art, neon, sci-fi, steam engines, heroic perspective, giant UI panels, watermarks, logos, unrelated decorations.
